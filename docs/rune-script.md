# Rune Script

Version 1 · 2026-11-08 · Approved first pass. Drawn on the "Rune script · first pass" artboard in the Spell Bar Prototype canvas.

Every root in the Starter Spell Lexicon has a rune built from a small kit of simple strokes, so players learn a script rather than 47 unrelated pictures.

## Rules

**Frame = grammar role**
- Source (what a spell is made of): circle, gold ink.
- Form (what it does): square, blue ink.
- Modifier (how it's tuned): diamond, violet ink. Modifiers carry no family mark.

**Family mark = lexicon group**
- Elements: ground line at the base (Umbra hangs it from the top instead).
- Force and motion: built from arrows and direction.
- Combat: blade wedge on top.
- Afflictions: a bead hanging beneath.
- Change and creation: a vessel underneath.
- Utility: a key notch in the top-left corner.
- Cosmic and moral: no mark; drawn as mirrored pairs.

**Opposites mirror or open:** Lum/Umbra, Vita/Nex, Cael/Orcus, Aug/Minu and Puls/Tract are mirror images. Sera/Rese are the same padlock, closed and open.

**Deliberate rule-breaker:** Aetern is an infinity mark in gold with a double diamond frame.

## Decisions from review

- Ignis: a flame with an inner tongue, sitting on the ground line.
- Fract: a slab split by a jagged crack.
- Vola: two spread, feathered wings.
- Punct: a spear driven through a broken barrier.
- Sangu: the only blood drop in the set, with a glint; final art needs more polish.
- Venen: a poison bottle marked with an X, so it no longer resembles Sangu.
- Sera: closed padlock. Rese: the same padlock open.
- Vinc: a chain of three links.
- Torp: a rigid figure pinned between posts. Alternative if it doesn't read: a figure frozen in cracked ice.
- Prec: praying hands with a small glow above.

## Later
